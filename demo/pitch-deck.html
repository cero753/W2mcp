Why it's recurring, not one-time

It stays correct, and it compounds.

Drift-maintenance

APIs change; hand-built connectors rot. w2mcp drift diffs the live source against the baseline (by method+path) and --fix regenerates. We own "keeping the integration alive" — the reason iPaaS is sold annually.

Self-improving generation

Every verified generation caches that API's real base-URL & auth, fed back as hints to future runs. The more APIs wired in, the better and faster generation gets — a data-compounding moat on top of spec-less extraction.

Guardrailed: learns only from live-verified runs, advisory not authoritative, kill-switch, spec path stays deterministic.
